# What Happened, and Why XRP Is Up Anyway

**Written:** 22 September 2026, ~6:45pm ET
**Question:** "Why is XRP finally going up when you said to sell everything before CLARITY because the
price was going down? I didn't listen because I believe in holding XRP. What's going on?"

---

## The short version

1. **The bad news I warned about happened.** Cloture failed 49–50 on 15 Sept. The Fed raised rates on
   16 Sept, its first hike since 2023. XRP went from **$1.49 to $1.28 (−14%)** in about a day.
2. **Then something outside every model took over.** Oil fell four sessions straight on Strait of Hormuz
   relief, traders who were short got squeezed (**~$650–770M of short liquidations**), and the SEC gave
   crypto something without waiting for Congress. Bitcoin went from ~$75k to **~$86k**, its highest level
   since January.
3. **XRP rode that tide.** It hasn't broken away on its own. It is up **+21% from the low** because it
   moves ~1.4× Bitcoin in both directions. Since 9 Sept it is up **+11.5%**, the same as ETH (+10.5%) and
   BTC (+9.7%), and *less* than your SOL (+14.5%).
4. **Against Bitcoin, XRP is ~7% lower than on the morning of the vote.** In dollars it is up; measured
   against the rest of the market it lost ground.

## Part 1 — Setting the record straight on what I said

You remember "sell everything, 80% it goes down." Here is what I actually said, in order:

| Date | What I said | Did it happen? |
|---|---|---|
| **5 Sept** | Sell ~$3,000 of XRP into savings, then ~$2,000 more into BTC/ETH. "At least one of the two events goes against you: **92%**." | Both did go against you. **But selling would have cost ~$270 so far on the first tranche**, maybe $300–500 for the whole plan. |
| **9 Sept** | Plan revised to **no selling**. Grow everything else around XRP instead. | You followed this. |
| **15 Sept, 00:43** | **Hold. Don't buy, don't sell.** ~35% chance the portfolio is up by Wednesday. Cloture fails: 55%. | Cloture failed. By Wednesday you were down about **−$690** on XRP alone. The median forecast was −$597. |

So three things are true at once:

- **The forecast was right about the 48 hours it covered.** The likely branch happened, and XRP
  fell ~8× harder than Bitcoin, exactly as the model said it would.
- **The early sell advice would have cost you money so far.** I own that. The 5 Sept plan was built
  on the next two weeks, and the next two weeks included a rally no one priced.
- **The last thing I told you before the vote was "hold," and you held.** You didn't disobey me on
  the final call. You disobeyed me on the 5 Sept call and were right to by ~$300 so far.

**The lesson isn't that conviction beats analysis.** You held through a 14% drop on a known-bad
event and then got bailed out by oil and a short squeeze. That was a good outcome from a
coin-flip-quality decision. Traders call this **"resulting"**: judging a decision by how it turned out
rather than by what was knowable at the time. If you take the lesson "always hold XRP through
bad news," the next time the tide doesn't come in, it will cost you far more than $300.

### How the vote went

- **49 yes, 50 no.** Three Republicans voted no: **Collins, Hawley and Moran.** Tillis also voted no
  as a procedural move, so he could file a **motion to reconsider**, which he did. That keeps the
  bill technically alive.
- **Every Democrat voted no, including all seven negotiators:** Alsobrooks, Booker, Cortez Masto,
  Gallego, Gillibrand, Warner and Warnock. The SEC/CFTC market-structure framework was essentially
  agreed. The bill died over **ethics language on the President's and his family's crypto holdings**.
- **Odds of it becoming law in 2026 are now 5.9% on Polymarket and 8.4% on Kalshi.** They were 82%
  in February and ~28% the night before the vote. Next Senate work period: **5 Oct – 6 Nov**. No new
  vote is scheduled. The realistic windows are the lame-duck session after the 3 Nov midterms, or 2027.
- Fairshake, the crypto PAC Ripple helps fund, has launched a **$30M** campaign against Sherrod Brown.
  The industry is now fighting this through the midterms, not the Senate floor.

## Part 3 — Why it went up after bad news: the five drivers, ranked

### 1. Oil and the Fed (the big one, and it's macro)
The Fed hiked because of inflation, and inflation is high because of the Iran war and the Strait of
Hormuz. From 16 Sept, oil fell four sessions in a row: WTI below ~$98, flows through Hormuz at a
six-month high, and Trump signalling he's open to meeting Iran's president at the UN this week.
**Cheaper oil means less inflation, which means fewer future hikes.** The market is betting on fewer
hikes than it feared a week ago. CME FedWatch on 17 Sept had October as a coin flip: **49% hold, 51% hike**. It still puts
~88% on at least one more hike by December.

### 2. A crowded short got squeezed (the fuel)
**Funding on XRP perpetual futures had turned negative *before* the vote.** That means traders were
paying to bet against it, so the failure was largely priced in. When prices ticked up instead, those
shorts were force-bought out. **~$650–770M of liquidations in 24 hours, ~85% of them shorts.** XRP
open interest is **$3.66B**. A squeeze makes a fast move, but not necessarily a lasting one.

### 3. Regulators went around Congress
Two days after the vote, the SEC issued a **five-year innovation exemption** for trading tokenized US
stocks on crypto rails, and the CFTC issued its own guidance. The market read it as **"we don't need
CLARITY to get a friendly regime."** For XRP specifically, the SEC–CFTC commodity treatment and the
final Ripple settlement still stand. The bill would have **written those into law**, and losing that
is what the −14% priced in. Nothing that exists today was taken away.

### 4. Big holders bought the dip
On-chain trackers show large XRP wallets growing from ~8.1B to ~9.7B XRP between 16 and 19 Sept, the
biggest spike on the 16th–17th, right at the low. **Treat this with caution:** exchange wallets get
mislabelled as whales, and the same data shows large XRP deposits *into* Binance, which is usually
selling pressure.

### 5. XRP-specific news (real, but small)
Ripple received its full EU **MiCA** licence. Absa launched XRP custody. The Moscow Exchange is listing
XRP perpetual futures. These are good for the long-run case and **didn't cause this week's move**.

### What *isn't* driving it: the ETFs
Spot XRP ETFs took in only **~$10–13M the week of the vote.** Compare **$110M** the week ending 28 Aug.
Cumulative flows (~$1.7B) have been stuck for three weeks. **Institutions are not piling into XRP here.
Leveraged traders and the macro tide are.**

## Part 5 — What I'd do now (mentor mode)

**1. Don't let the bounce reset the plan.** Nothing about the fundamental case changed this week: no
law, higher rates, ETF buying stalled. What changed is oil and positioning, and both can reverse in a
day. One headline out of Hormuz undoes the entire driver #1.

**2. Know the levels before you get there.**

| Level | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **$1.55–1.60** | **~2.5B XRP last traded here.** Lots of holders like you are waiting to "get back to even." That's why it's resistance. | Nothing. Watch whether it closes the *week* above $1.60. |
| $1.68 / $1.86 | Next supply clusters. $1.88 ≈ the 18-month average. | — |
| **$1.93** | **Your Robinhood break-even.** | **The trap price.** Selling here just because it's even is the disposition effect, not a strategy. Decide on size, not on your cost. |
| **$1.28–1.30** | The post-vote low. The rally has failed if it closes below this. | Don't add. Hold (no forced sale; you have runway). |
| $1.05–1.10 | Deeper support | — |

**3. The one move that's better *because* XRP went up:** the rule we agreed was to **trim on size
(>20% of the book), not on gain %.** XRP is at 27% and rising. When you're ready, the right trim
comes from **Kraken first** (basis unknown, find it) or from lots held **>1 year** (0% long-term rate
at your income). **Not** from Robinhood lots at a loss just to feel even. Moving 5–7 points of XRP into
**BTC** keeps you in crypto, keeps you in the same rally, and cuts the single-token risk. You don't
need to sell to cash.

**4. Don't add to XRP on this bounce.** "No more XRP buys" from 9 Sept still stands. Buying after a
+21% squeeze with October hike odds at 50/50 is chasing.

**5. Still the biggest lever:** the job. With no income, the concentration can't be diluted by new
money. Every trade above is small next to $500/month going into an index fund.

## Honest scorecard on my own calls

- **Right:** that cloture would likely fail (55%); that XRP would fall far harder than BTC; the
  size of the Wednesday loss (−$690 vs a −$597 median); that "dead until 2029" was overstated (the
  motion to reconsider is live).
- **Wrong or missing:** the 5 Sept sell plan cost ~$270+ so far. **No model had the oil reversal or the
  squeeze in it.** A two-day event model tells you nothing about day seven.
- **Standing view unchanged:** XRP probably rises over 3 years (~60% to clear $1.90), probably lags
  Bitcoin (~25% it beats it), and 27% of net worth in one token is too much at any price.
