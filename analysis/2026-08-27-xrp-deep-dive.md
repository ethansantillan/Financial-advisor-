# XRP Deep Dive — Research Note, 27 August 2026

**Price $1.42 · Market cap ~$86.6B · 61B circulating, 38.2B escrowed · ATH $3.84 (Jan 2018)**

## The call

> A speculative asset with a strong retail base, a weakening utility thesis, and a large scheduled
> supply overhang — attached to a genuinely good company whose success does not reliably reach it.

The bull case is almost entirely **regulatory and reflexive** (CLARITY, ETF flows, the retail bid),
not utility-driven. That isn't disqualifying — Bitcoin's case isn't utility-driven either. It means
XRP should be sized as *a speculation on legislation and flows*, not as a bet on Ripple's business.
Those are different investments and only one is available to buy.

## 1. The finding: two assets, one ticker

**Ripple the company — executing**
- Targeting a **$1B revenue run rate** in 2026 — a target stated *excluding XRP*
- Bought prime broker **Hidden Road for $1.25B**; ~$3T annual clearing volume
- **RLUSD $132M → $2B+** in ~18 months; fastest regulated stablecoin to $1B (under 120 days)
- Payments live in 40+ corridors, 300+ RippleNet institutions
- **OCC conditional approval** for a crypto trust bank charter; named in Mastercard's AI-payments push
- Swell merged with XRPL Apex — NYC, **27–29 October**

**XRP the token — softening**
- **Ten major Ripple deals closed in 2026. None settled in XRP.**
- XRPL Q2: transactions **−6.5% QoQ**, DEX volume **−35.9% QoQ**, active addresses **−10.7% QoQ**
- **24.6% of Q2 transactions failed** (54.6M of 222.4M)
- Everything tokenized on XRPL totals **$980M** — ~1.1% of XRP's own market cap
- Seven spot ETFs hold ~**$1.4B** (1.6% of cap) — and are **84% retail**
- **38.2B XRP still escrowed** = 63% of circulating supply

RLUSD — Ripple's own stablecoin — is becoming the preferred settlement asset for institutional
business. Ripple found its customers want dollars, not a volatile bridge, and built them dollars.
Commercially correct; awkward for token holders specifically.

## 2. Does Ripple's success reach the token?

**The bull answer, fairly stated:** RLUSD and XRP are complementary, not competing. RLUSD is the unit
of account on XRPL pairs while XRP bridges between RLUSD and everything else, so every mint and
redemption is an XRP-denominated event. Stablecoin throughput becomes XRP throughput. Nearly half of
RLUSD's $2B sits on the XRPL, which supports the picture.

**My problem:** it's a very *thin* mechanism. XRPL fees are deliberately near-zero and flat, so XRP
consumed per settlement is negligible. A mechanism routing billions of stablecoin activity through
fractions of a cent of XRP demand doesn't underwrite an $86.6B valuation. Volume passing *through* an
asset ≠ demand *to hold* it.

Even bullish 2026 coverage concedes it: XRP responds to direct XRP demand (ETF inflows, on-chain
activity, retail accumulation), not to Ripple's enterprise success.

### Valuation gap in ratios

| XRPL network fees, YTD 2026 | <$120K | 721,000× | Near-zero **by design** — XRPL burns a flat fee, no fee market. Not proof of no usage. But the network captures no value, throwing valuation entirely onto demand-to-hold. |

## 3. The supply overhang

**38.2B XRP escrowed = $54.2B = 63% of circulating supply.** 1B unlocks monthly; Ripple re-escrows
600–800M and keeps the rest. That retained ~300M/month is **$5.1B/year ≈ 5.9% of market cap annually**
— and Ripple genuinely spends it: grants, ecosystem investment, salaries, legal bills, and
acquisitions like the $1.25B Hidden Road deal.

> **Ripple succeeding is partially financed by selling the thing you own.** Not a scandal — it's the
> disclosed, mechanical design. But it's the opposite of equity, where success accrues to holders
> rather than being paid for by them.

Original guidance had escrow largely depleted by ~2027; at current net rates the runway is closer to
**six to ten more years**.

## 4. The binary: 15 September

Nearly every serious bull case routes through the **Digital Asset Market Clarity Act**.

- Thune filed cloture; **15 September procedural vote**, needs **60 votes**
- Coverage calls its chances **"hanging by a thread"**
- Only **14 working days** between the 14 Sept return and the October election recess; 22 through year-end
- Full House and a third of the Senate on November ballots — floor time will be scarce
- Sticking points are partisan and real: Democrats want ethics/illicit-finance safeguards; banks
  object to exchanges paying yield on stablecoin balances
- **A failed cloture vote could effectively kill it**

You have a dated, binary, thesis-defining event 19 days out, and "CLARITY dies in the Senate" is
already in your written invalidation rules. **15 September is the day that rule fires or doesn't.**

## 5. The strongest bull case

- **Legislation is a step-function, not a trend.** If CLARITY passes, XRP gets permanent commodity
  status and everyone waiting for exactly that can act at once. 18% of surveyed institutions already
  hold XRP; 25% plan to add in 2026. Nothing on-chain would have predicted that re-rating.
- **Spot ETFs didn't exist in prior cycles.** $1.4B cumulative is a genuinely new structural bid.
- **Ripple's institutional position is its strongest ever** — OCC charter approval, RLUSD under dual
  OCC/NYDFS supervision, Mastercard, Hidden Road's $3T footprint. If any of it routes demand to the
  token, the token is small relative to those flows.
- **The retail base is real** — ~8M wallets, 20M+ users, fastest-growing cohort holds 1,000–10,000 XRP.
  In a bull market that base bids and no model captures it.
- **The fee critique cuts less than it looks.** Near-zero flat fees are deliberate payment-rail
  architecture; 222M transactions a quarter is real throughput.
- **Swell, 27–29 October** is a scheduled venue for exactly the announcements that have moved it before.

Weight legislation and reflexivity heavily, discount on-chain metrics as noise, and you get a genuinely
constructive view. It isn't stupid. It's a bet on Congress and on flows, and should be labeled as one.

## 6. My assessment

**~25–30% that XRP trades at $3+ within three years.** I came in at roughly one in three and came out
slightly *lower*. Three things did it, none on my radar beforehand: the ten-deals-none-in-XRP
datapoint, the across-the-board Q2 on-chain decline, and how precarious CLARITY looks on the calendar.

Escrow arithmetic means $3 in 2029 needs a **~$210B cap** against ~70B tokens — returning to its
all-time-peak valuation, not exceeding it. It failed to make a new high through two complete bull
cycles while Bitcoin made new highs in both.

> **The question that decides the position isn't "does XRP reach $3" — it's "does XRP beat Bitcoin."**
> I put that at roughly **one in four**. Nothing here moved it up. The clearest single fact in the file
> is that Ripple, which knows more about what institutions want than anyone, keeps choosing to settle
> its own biggest deals in something other than XRP.

**What I'd do:** hold a position, not a concentration. Everything here is consistent with XRP as a
legitimate **10–20% speculative sleeve**. Almost none of it supports 52%. The research didn't change my
view of what XRP *is* — it lowered my confidence that the mechanism you're counting on actually works.

## 8. How much to trust this

**This is not institutional-grade research and shouldn't be read as such.** Crypto media is thin,
frequently promotional and often conflicted; several sources carried obvious directional bias and
price-prediction pages are close to worthless. I leaned on more accountable outlets (CoinDesk, Motley
Fool, Congress.gov, Ripple's own materials) and treated exchange blogs and SEO pages as leads to
verify rather than facts.

Specific weaknesses: **I could not independently verify the "ten deals, none in XRP" claim against
Ripple's own disclosures, and it does a lot of work in my conclusion — if it's wrong, this note is
materially weaker.** Escrow, on-chain and legislative figures are better sourced. I have no live market
feed, so every price is approximate and time-lagged.

The probabilities are my judgement, not model output. Nobody can price a Senate vote and a three-year
crypto cycle to a percentage. Treat them as a statement of conviction.
