## Where I could be wrong

- **Prediction markets aren't oracles.** 82% in February, 18% now. Thin markets move on small money.
- **Cloture failing isn't the bill dying.** Leadership can renegotiate and refile.
- **The Fed may not hike.** 60–66% isn't 100%, and analysts are split on whether Warsh endorsed a move.
  A hold reads dovish and could rally everything.
- **Bernstein wrote those numbers near BTC $65,000.** It's $77,155 now, so $55–60K is a *steeper* fall
  from here than "10–25%" suggests — closer to −22% to −29%. I used their percentages, which may be
  conservative.
- **It may already be priced.** Markets often sell the rumour and buy the news.
- **My prices are 1–3 days old**, from published pages not a live feed.

None of that changes the direction, because **the plan doesn't need the forecast to be right.** Selling
$3,000 into an emergency fund is correct whether the bill passes, fails, or gets delayed again. The
events only make the timing urgent.
