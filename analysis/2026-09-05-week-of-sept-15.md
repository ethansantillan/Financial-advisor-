## The week itself — two binaries, 24 hours apart

**Tue 15 Sept, 2:15pm ET — CLARITY Act cloture vote.** Needs 60. Would split SEC/CFTC oversight and give
XRP permanent commodity status. **Polymarket: 18%.** A failed cloture effectively pushes market-structure
legislation to 2027.

**Wed 16 Sept, 2:00pm ET — FOMC decision.** The one nobody told you about. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's
28 August Jackson Hole speech was unexpectedly hawkish — better summer inflation readings "do not tell
him underlying trends have meaningfully improved." **CME FedWatch: 60–66% chance of a 25bp HIKE**, up
from ~35%. It would be the **first increase since July 2023.** Three of five 2026 Fed decision days were
already clear bearish pivots for Bitcoin.

## The vote math

| The arithmetic | Votes |

| Needed for cloture | 60 |

| Expected defections *(Hawley, Paul, possibly Tillis)* | −3 |
| **Democrats needed** | **10+** |

| …and both now **oppose** this version | **0** |

Seven Democratic senators signed a joint statement that the draft "falls short." Three unresolved
fights: **ethics rules** covering the President's ~$1.4B crypto income (Democrats object to the
carve-out); **DeFi developer liability** under Section 604; and a **stablecoin yield provision**
threatening ~$1.35B of annual Coinbase USDC rewards revenue.

It cleared the House 294–134 and Senate Banking 15–9, so it isn't dead. But needing ten Democrats when
your only two crossovers have publicly turned against the text, ten days out — **18% may be generous.**

## Where I could be wrong

- **Prediction markets aren't oracles.** 82% in February, 18% now. Thin markets move on small money.
- **Cloture failing isn't the bill dying.** Leadership can renegotiate and refile.
- **The Fed may not hike.** 60–66% isn't 100%, and analysts are split on whether Warsh endorsed a move.
  A hold reads dovish and could rally everything.
- **Bernstein wrote those numbers near BTC $65,000.** It's $77,155 now, so $55–60K is a *steeper* fall
  from here than "10–25%" suggests — closer to −22% to −29%. I used their percentages, which may be
  conservative.
- **It may already be priced.** Markets often sell the rumour and buy the news.
- **My prices are 1–3 days old**, from published pages not a live feed.

None of that changes the direction, because **the plan doesn't need the forecast to be right.** Selling
$3,000 into an emergency fund is correct whether the bill passes, fails, or gets delayed again. The
events only make the timing urgent.
