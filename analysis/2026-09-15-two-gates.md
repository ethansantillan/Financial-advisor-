## 1. Gate 1 — CLARITY cloture, Tue 15 Sep, 2:15 PM ET

Cloture on the **motion to proceed** — whether the Senate takes up H.R. 3633 at all.
Not passage, not even floor debate on the text. Clearing it opens amendments; failing it
ends CLARITY for 2026 and, on midterm math, likely until 2029.

**Prediction markets: ~30–35% on 2026 passage**, roughly double the high-teens/low-20s of
a week ago. The move came from a White House concession on 13–14 Sep: federal officials
and spouses must divest digital assets or use blind trusts, with civil penalties of
$500,000 or 20% of transaction value, enforceable by DOJ *and* state AGs. Reported as
~80% of the ethics provisions Democrats asked for. The finalized 635-page draft landed
14 Sep from Lummis, Boozman and Scott.

**Seat arithmetic:**
- 60 needed. Republicans hold 53.
- Defections: Paul, Hawley, possibly Tillis → **~50 Republican yes**.
- **9–10 Democrats required.** About a dozen have negotiated for months; **as of Friday no
  published whip count reaches 60.**
- Schumer caucused Democrats Sunday evening. Many argue the ethics language still fails to
  reach the President's family ventures, and it arrived <48 hours before the vote.

## 2. Gate 2 — FOMC, Wed 16 Sep — and it is a *hike*

This is the underweighted risk in the whole engagement.

August CPI (released 11 Sep): **3.4% annual**, core **+0.3% m/m** — both hotter than
forecast. Traders now price **~90% odds of a 25bp increase**, which would be the **first
hike since 2023**, with **two hikes fully priced by year-end**. Chair Warsh is under
public pressure to move. The meeting also carries updated projections and a new dot plot.

Crypto's entire 2023–2026 bull case rested on an easing cycle. A Fed that has stopped
cutting and started hiking re-rates every long-duration risk asset in the book — crypto,
NVDA, META, and the whole 21-ticker AI list.
