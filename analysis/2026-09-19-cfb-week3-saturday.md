## Tonight: the same setup, in a better game

The Friday winner was a high-target **slot receiver** against a secondary with a
**public, demonstrated coverage failure**. That exact configuration is on the board again.

### The matchup: No. 7 LSU at No. 8 Ole Miss — 4:30pm PT, ABC, Oxford

LSU −3, total 57.5-58.5. **The highest total on the night board, on a three-point
spread** — which is the ideal prop environment: no blowout, starters play four quarters,
and both offenses are forced to keep throwing.

**Ole Miss's secondary is tonight's Texas Tech.** They rank **129th nationally in
coverage grade** and **132nd in PFF tackle grading**, and in the opener Louisville's

into 162 yards.

**Winston Watkins Jr. is the man who attacks it.** LSU's starting slot receiver, leads
the team in targets, **double-digit targets in both games** (10 targets / 7 catches vs
Clemson; 7 catches, 110 yards, TD vs Louisiana Tech). Scouted as "a twitched-up slot
receiver with burst and short-area quickness" — a YAC player, against the 132nd-ranked
tackling team in the country. Line 53.5.

**Sam Leavitt** is healthy (removed from the availability report entirely), 574 passing
yards and nine total TDs in two games, 287 ypg. Line 271.5.

## The slip

| Leg | Book line | Shade to | Projection | Est. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sam Leavitt OVER pass yds** (LSU) | 271.5 | **~219.5** | ~285 | ~78% |
| **Winston Watkins Jr. OVER rec yds** (LSU) | 53.5 | **~39.5** | ~75 | ~80% |
| **Ryan Browne OVER pass yds** (Purdue) | ~280 | **~239.5** | ~305 | ~80% |

Legs 1 and 2 are the **same bet twice** — a quarterback's passing yards are the sum of
his receivers' yards, so Leavitt throwing means Watkins catching. That is the structure
that produced Friday's second slip (Weigman + Thomas), and it is free money whenever the
combo engine multiplies the legs independently. Leg 3 is a different game at a different
hour, so it is genuinely independent.

Independent product **49.9%**; the correlation on legs 1-2 lifts true joint to **~54%**.
Against a 22% break-even with a 50% boost, that is the strongest slip of the two days.

**Stake $10 with a boost.** The boost is still the entire edge — it drops the required
per-leg rate from 66.7% to 60.4%, and all three legs sit near 80%. Never run it unboosted.

### Deliberately not in the slip

- **Two LSU receivers together.** Watkins and TE Trey'Dez Green (6'7", 104 yards and a TD
  on nine targets last week, leads LSU in yards per route run) both beat Ole Miss's
  coverage — but they compete for the same targets. Same reasoning that kept Hughes out

- **Any Ole Miss pass-catcher.** Deuce Alexander has cleared 71.5 in both games (76, then

  **the top coverage unit in FBS**, top-10 in havoc, success rate allowed and quality
  drives allowed, with EDGE Princewill Umanmielen at 10 pressures and five sacks. Betting
  into the best coverage defense in the country to chase one weak link is not the trade.
- **Trinidad Chambliss over 277.5.** Same reason. He has cleared 282 in 10 of 15 starts,
  but none of those were against this defense.
- **Blowout games.** Notre Dame −29.5, Texas −29.5, Washington −35.5, Tennessee −35.5,
  Oklahoma −22.5. Starters sit; skill props die in the third quarter. Friday's Oregon
  call was right and applies to all of these.
