## The slip

| Leg | Book line | Shade to | Projection | Est. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sam Leavitt OVER pass yds** (LSU) | 271.5 | **~219.5** | ~285 | ~78% |
| **Winston Watkins Jr. OVER rec yds** (LSU) | 53.5 | **~39.5** | ~75 | ~80% |
| **Ryan Browne OVER pass yds** (Purdue) | ~280 | **~239.5** | ~305 | ~80% |

Legs 1 and 2 are the **same bet twice** — a quarterback's passing yards are the sum of
his receivers' yards, so Leavitt throwing means Watkins catching. That is the structure
that produced Friday's second slip (Weigman + Thomas), and it is free money whenever the
combo engine multiplies the legs independently. Leg 3 is a different game at a different
hour, so it is genuinely independent.

Independent product **49.9%**; the correlation on legs 1-2 lifts true joint to **~54%**.
Against a 22% break-even with a 50% boost, that is the strongest slip of the two days.

**Stake $10 with a boost.** The boost is still the entire edge — it drops the required
per-leg rate from 66.7% to 60.4%, and all three legs sit near 80%. Never run it unboosted.

# REVISION — 2:00pm PT: deeper research cuts a leg and lowers two numbers

Asked to dig further before locking the slip. Good call: **two of the three legs had
problems that the first pass missed.**

## 🚨 Cut: Sam Leavitt over passing yards

Three separate findings, any one of which is disqualifying for a **quarterback yardage**
prop:

1. **He has a sore throwing shoulder.** He was listed **DOUBTFUL on Wednesday's
   availability report**, upgraded to questionable Thursday, and only cleared to start on
   Friday. He was limited in practice all week.
2. **His efficiency is worse than the headline.** The "574 yards, nine total TDs" line
   flattered him. The real split: **62.1% completion, 574 yards, two passing touchdowns
   and FOUR interceptions.** His scoring is coming on the ground — five rushing TDs, tied
   for the most of any SEC player. He threw three picks against *Louisiana Tech*.
3. **LSU doesn't need him to throw.** 456 rushing yards in two games, and Leavitt himself
   has 135 rushing yards. Backup Landen Clark (Elon transfer) is 7-of-10 for 109 yards
   with a TD and no picks, so there is a usable relief option if the shoulder flares.

Add the venue: **Oxford is under a Heat Advisory, high near 98°F**, sunny, calm wind, with
the advisory running to 8:00pm and kickoff at 6:30pm CT. Extreme heat pushes teams toward
the run and shortens possessions in the first half.

And the scheme cuts the wrong way here. Kiffin's offense has run a **play-action rate
above 30% for three straight seasons** and pushes the ball vertically — the deep shot is
precisely the throw a sore shoulder cannot make. Betting a yardage total on a quarterback
who was doubtful 72 hours ago, in 98-degree heat, on a run-first team, is avoidable risk.
**Cut.**

## ✅ Keep: Winston Watkins Jr. — but shade lower than planned

The thesis survives and actually strengthens, because **the slot is the one role a sore
shoulder helps.** A limited quarterback throws short, quick and underneath — which is
exactly Watkins's route tree. He is insulated from the injury that kills the Leavitt leg.

**The game log, which the first pass had wrong:**

| | Targets | Rec | Yards |
|---|---|---|---|
| vs Clemson | **11** | 7 | **110** |
| vs Louisiana Tech | **9** | 5 | **43** |

Twenty targets in two games leads LSU — the volume is elite and consistent. But **the
floor game was 43 yards**, and his line is 53.5. He went *under* it last week. A shade to
39.5 leaves only a 3.5-yard margin against his own floor, which is too thin.
**Shade to ~34.5**, which clears both games comfortably.

**What's in his favor:** Ole Miss is **129th in coverage grade and 132nd in PFF tackle
grading**, and Louisville hit them for **seven completions of 20+ yards** in the opener.
A short-area slot receiver against a team that cannot tackle is a YAC engine — the same
structure that turned Amare Thomas into 162 yards on Friday.

**And the storyline is real.** Lane Kiffin left Ole Miss for LSU before the Rebels' playoff
run and returns to Oxford tonight as a declared villain — enhanced law enforcement, LSU
gear banned from the student section. An LSU source to On3: *"He doesn't just want to beat
these guys. He wants to embarrass them."* **Watkins is himself one of three former Rebels
on LSU's roster.** Kiffin also knows Ole Miss head coach Pete Golding's defense intimately
— Golding was his own defensive coordinator. None of that is a number, but none of it
argues for LSU taking its foot off the gas.

## ⚠️ Keep Ryan Browne — but shade well below the plan

**UCLA's secondary is considerably better than the "44th nationally" figure implied:**

- They held San Diego State to **45 passing yards** — the fewest UCLA has allowed in a
  game **since 2017**. SDSU's quarterback went 6-of-17.
- Corner **Ta'Shawn James has two interceptions and is the reigning Big Ten Defensive
  Player of the Week.**

That is not the soft spot the first pass assumed. The leg survives on **volume, not
matchup**: Browne is 55-of-71 (**77.5%**) for 646 yards with four TDs and **zero
interceptions**, leads the Big Ten in *both* passing yards (323/gm) and completions
(27.5/gm), and owns **the longest active 300+ yard passing streak in FBS**. As a
two-touchdown underdog he is scripted into throwing.

**Shade from ~280 down to ~219.5.** He would need a 32% drop from his season average to
miss — enough cushion to absorb a good secondary. Rose Bowl weather is 74°F at kickoff,
no factor.
