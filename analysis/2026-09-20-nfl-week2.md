## Two leads died on research

**Jaxon Smith-Njigba — CUT.** The 79.5 line looked soft against 122 yards and a 45.8%
target share in Week 1. Then: **Sam Darnold is expected to miss Week 2 and Drew Lock
starts.** That is why a dome game carries a **41.5 total**, second-lowest on the board.
Seattle is also favored, so there is no trailing script to force volume, and JSN's career
rate over 80 yards is **40% (22 of 55 games)**. The market has the backup quarterback
priced; the line is not soft, it is correct.

**Every Miami pass-catcher — CUT.** Jaylen Waddle was traded to Denver in March and
Tyreek Hill was released in February; the receiving room is Jalen Tolbert and Malik
Washington. **Malik Willis** is the quarterback. They are **+13.5** against a San
Francisco defense that held Stafford to seven points with two takeaways, with Bosa and
Warner healthy. Garbage-time volume is not worth that.

## The replacement is better than either

### Justin Jefferson — Vikings at Bears, 10:00 PT

**Chicago is allowing the second-most passing yards in the NFL, 353 per game**, and just
gave up **37 points to Carolina**. Their corners were shredded in Week 1: Jaylon Johnson
allowed 75 yards to Tet McMillan, Tyrique Stevenson allowed 138 to Jalen Coker.

Jefferson's side of it:
- Week 1: **8 catches, 92 yards, 2 TD on 9 targets**
- **83 receiving yards per game against the Bears since 2021**
- **With Carson Wentz starting he has cleared his receiving prop in every single start** —
  averaging **10 targets, 7 receptions, 95 yards** per game
- Lines seen: 73.5 at books, 70+ at $0.59-0.61 on Kalshi, model projection 83

**The risk is weather, and it is real.** Soldier Field is forecast for **13-32 mph wind
with rain probability around 72%**. Wind suppresses the deep ball and typically knocks
10-15% off passing output. That is exactly why this is a *shaded* play rather than a
take-the-number play — at a rung in the high 50s, even a wind-suppressed Jefferson clears.

### Stefon Diggs — Commanders at Cowboys, 1:25 PT

**Dallas allows the most receiving yards to wide receivers in the NFL — 171 per game.**
Week 1 showed the mechanism: when the Cowboys rushed four, Jaxson Dart went **17-of-19
for 200 yards and two touchdowns**, and they surrendered **215 yards in nickel on 24
snaps**. This is the **highest total on the board at 50.5**, and Washington is a **+4.5
underdog**, which is the trailing script that adds attempts.

Diggs's line is **47.5** and he has averaged **53.0 adjusted yards per game** since the
start of last season. He **led Washington in targets in Week 1** (55 yards, TD).

**The risk:** Washington's targets are genuinely fragmented. In Week 1 rookie Antonio
Williams led the team in receiving yards and **Terry McLaurin saw only four targets**.
Diggs could see five looks and finish at 35.

- **Chicago wind.** If it drops under ~12 mph the Jefferson leg gets better; if gusts hit
  the top of the 32 mph range, shade lower still or drop it.
- **Carson Wentz's status.** The entire Jefferson volume case rests on Wentz starting.
- **Washington's Week 2 target distribution.** If beat reporting says Antonio Williams is
  being elevated again, drop Diggs.
- **Sam Darnold.** If he is activated, Seattle's total moves and JSN comes back into play.
- Confirmed already: Seattle's JSN and Cooper Kupp both practiced 16 Sept; SF's
  McCaffrey, Kittle and Bosa all full practice; Miami's Chop Robinson OUT (concussion).
