## Questions to ask (top 6 in order; skip any he's already answered)
1. **"If you were 23 again with a business degree and no sales experience, what first job would you take?"**
2. **The SDR question:** "I'm weighing three kinds of first jobs. One is SDR/BDR: in an office on the phone, prospecting and setting meetings for closers, like a tech SDR or Shamrock's BDR role in Fort Lauderdale. Two is outside sales with a territory, like CED or a company in Austin. Three is medical, like Medline's associate program. Which would you pick for someone like me, and why?"
3. **Pharma:** "You spent eight years at Pfizer. How did you get in, what was it really like, and is pharma still a good path today? Would you do it again?"
4. **Ag:** "You started in Yuba City, right by where I grew up. Is ag sales a real option for a business grad without an ag degree? Do you need the PCA, and are there roles that don't?"
5. **Hiring manager view:** "You've managed reps. What makes you say yes to someone with no experience? What are the red flags?"
6. **CED Tuesday:** "I have an in-person with the GM of CED Stockton on Tuesday. You ran a distribution sales team at xpedx. What would you want to hear from me in that meeting?"

**If there's time:** "What separates the reps who make real money from the ones who don't?" · "What do you wish someone had told you at my age?"

**Always end with these two:**
- "Is there anyone else you think I should talk to?"
- "Would you be willing to look at my resume and tell me what you'd change?"

**Don't ask for a job at Redox on this call.** If he offers help or an intro, say yes and thank him.
