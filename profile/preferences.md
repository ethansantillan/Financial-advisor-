*Confirmed by Ethan, 29 Sep 2026.*

> "A sales job that gives me experience, and then I make good money. Salary and commission based. If I ever want to leave, I'll have great experience to get any sales job."

**The goal is a launchpad:** a salaried B2B sales role with real training and a quota, so that in 12–24 months he has a track record (quota attainment, rank) that gets him into higher-paying sales, including medical device or pharma.

## Hard filters

- **Commission-only, 1099, pay-per-lead, or "independent contractor" roles**: out.
- **Base salary below $50,000**: out. (A range that includes commission, like "$45k–$90k total", fails unless the base is stated at $50k+.)
- Part-time or contract: out (full-time only).

## Compensation

- **Base floor $50,000**, **target $70,000 base**, plus commission or bonus on top.
- **Relocation floor:** a relocating offer has to cover rent, since Ethan pays none now. Adjust the $50k floor by the destination's cost of living (BEA Regional Price Parities) and weigh relocation assistance heavily. Roughly: Texas and most of Florida need no adjustment above $50k; Miami, Austin, Denver, and similar metros ~$55k; Bay Area, LA, NYC, Boston ~$62k+.
