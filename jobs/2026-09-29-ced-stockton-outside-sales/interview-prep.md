## 2. What the job almost certainly is

Outside sales at an electrical distributor means:
- **Driving to customers:** electrical contractors' shops and jobsites, plant maintenance managers, maybe ag and food-processing customers around the Valley.
- **Getting on their bid lists:** quoting jobs (often from plans), following up on quotes, winning the order.
- **Being the fixer:** when material is late or wrong, you coordinate with the inside/counter team and the warehouse to get it to the jobsite.
- **Growing accounts:** more of each contractor's spend, new contractors. Pay is usually base + commission (often on gross profit) + car allowance or company vehicle.
- Many distributors start new reps **on the counter or inside sales for a few months** to learn the product. If Chris says that, it's normal and good.
