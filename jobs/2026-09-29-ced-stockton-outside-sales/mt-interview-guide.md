## 3. How an electrical distributor makes money
- Buy from manufacturers (Square D, Eaton, Siemens, Southwire…), stock it, sell to contractors and plants at a markup.
- **Gross profit (GP)** = sales − cost of the goods. **GP%** = GP ÷ sales. A branch is judged on sales, GP, expenses, inventory, and collections.
- **AR (accounts receivable):** money customers owe CED. Contractors usually buy on credit (often net 30), so collecting matters.
- **AP (accounts payable):** money CED owes vendors. Paying means matching the purchase order, what was received, and the invoice.
- **Inventory:** too much ties up cash; too little loses sales. **Turns** = how many times a year you sell through your stock.
- **Two kinds of business:** stock sales over the counter (will-call), and project business where a contractor bids a job from plans and the distributor quotes the whole material package.
- **Service is the product:** right material, right place, on time. A late delivery leaves a crew of electricians standing around.

- **Wiring devices:** switches, receptacles.
- **Lighting and controls.**
- **Motor control:** VFDs (variable frequency drives) control motor speed for pumps and fans; motor starters.
- **Tools and safety gear.**
